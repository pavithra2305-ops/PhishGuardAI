Safety checklist

  • Do not share passwords, one-time codes, or banking details in response to unexpected messages.
  • Check the sender and domain carefully; a familiar logo is not proof of authenticity.
  • Navigate to a service using its official app or a saved address rather than a message link.
  • If uncertain, verify through a trusted contact method.

Limitations: This is a rule-based educational demo, not a trained AI model, antivirus tool, or definitive phishing detector. A low-risk result does not mean a message is safe.
